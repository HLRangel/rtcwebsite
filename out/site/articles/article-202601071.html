(EN) RTC Audiostream Coming soon!

We'll provide a continuous bilingual audio stream, featuring music, commentary, news, and more.

We are proud to announce the creation of the RTC Audiophonic Services (rvi), an audio stream which will be served via social platforms such as YouTube and Twitch, along with icecast2 (internet radio). We also plan to provide brief streams on amateur radio bands in the future -- hopefully you'll be one of our lucky listeners there!.


The conversational portions of the stream will be bilingual, with segments in both Brazilian Portuguese and English. It will be a multifaceted cultural service, featuring audiobooks, original commentary, bits of news, and a collection of music in the Public Domain, all of which will also be accessible via the web.


Eventually, we plan to feature 24-hour service, with round-the-clock music and breaking news updates being delivered by an (obviously non-human) machine-generated voice.


The first test stream will occur on the 9th of January, 2026, 18:00 GMT-3. Check out our Bluesky and Xitter profiles and/or RTC contributor channels to find out where to listen!
