# THE MEETING PROCESS

## Location and Access

1. The meetings of the RTC may take place online or in person.

2. Meetings are open by default, with access governed by the ability of those interested to access the designated meeting location, observing the practices in Point XX.

3. When a meeting takes place by the grace and concession of another, in their home or establishment, within public recreational facilities and libraries, and/or within the time of a larger event, the alloted time shall be strictly respected and the Session adjourned in case its topics are not exhausted.
