Matters of the Third Session

Deferred: Paths for Disagreement Resolution on the Matter of the Strike Platform/Voting Platform

Discussion of this has been deferred until all those interested are present within the same session again.

Mailing List Discussion

A decision has been made to go forward with the mailing list. After examining a handful of services and attempting to find a good balance between ease-of-use and cost, we have settled on MailerSend as the service which will be used, all others have too much of an up-front cost in comparison with it, even if they are vastly cheaper per-email; we are not going to be sending enough messages to justify such cost.

A desirable division of expenses has been reached on this matter.

Git remote setup and project spreadsheet

We'll go forward with it, though we need a better way to figure out commit access than username/password pairs. Something can probably be done with SSH keys.

Repositories must be up for the following projects, so everyone is on the same page in regards to the work being done:

  • TBLOC
  • Faust site
  • RTC site
  • Ploogly

Need for system administrators

No one else is in a state to deal with the responsibility of superuser access. As such, we will have to create utilities which allow for certain repetitive administrative tasks, such as the creation of accounts and the automatic generation of documentation related to them. Additional discussion on this matter will happen shortly.

Proposal: Voting to certify

Lopes (paraphrased): It would be a nice little idea to have a final anonymous vote to ratify the conclusions reached in the Yearly Meeting. Anyone who participated in any of the sessions could register to vote.

Broad agreement

Project board (post-session)

It would be useful to have a kind of "project board", where people could ask for volunteers on new projects and otherwise just divulge them. This could be linked to the git in some way, though we shouldn't really emphasize it that much in order to not discourage non-programming/non-computing projects.

Translation Project (post-session)

We should create a voluntary roll of people available to translate texts, proceedings, etc. into a different language, with the ultimate goal of translating the whole website and allowing for international non-anglophone representation within the RTC. Translating the preamble and statute into as many languages as possible is the first priority.

Current events: Situation in Latin America

The United States' interests in Latin America have flared up again, especially in relation to Venezuela. It is not merely an inter-imperialist conflict with strongly outlined regional interests, and is instead a trove of unknown disasters depending on whether the North-American military offensive progresses to its logical conclusion: a full ground invasion.

Particularly, we are seeing a scenario where the stated motivations are an inversion of the true interests of the US, which possibly seeks to destabilize the region in order to leverage the inevitable post-invasion boom in the drug trade, weapons trade, and illegal mining against local enemies. In fact, US foreign policy in the past year has either intentionally or unintentionally succeeded in reducing the right-wing americanophiles in the rest of the continent to a servile state, reducing their electoral support but more deeply tying their remaining supporters to an openly collaborationist bourgeois project.

Of course, this strategy could crumble as quickly as it was developed. Its continued existence relies on a persistent myth about the Trump government and other precarious right-wing governments across the world, that there is a "method to the madness", either in the form of a master plan or in the form of internal factions being able to successfully steer things in their direction.

Current events: Epstein

This really needs no introduction. While it has been known for a long time that Jeffrey Epstein was a rapist and sex trafficker, the recently released pictures from the Special Redaction Project (also known as the "Epstein Library") bring into evidence a grim reality, showing to the minds of the public that Epstein, the power politics mediator and billionaire, and Epstein, the brutal rapist, are the same person.

While the current trend among Marxists is to look at the situation and say that the righteous hatred directed towards the accomplices of this sex trafficking operation are a direct manifestation of the class struggle, with the Revolutionary Communist International going so far as to appropriate the notion of the bourgeoisie as the "Epstein class", the actual source of the hatred is deeper.

After all, many of those horrified at the events on display find a firm political home across the spectrum of the bourgeois political establishment, from the right to the left. Many of them are, themselves, petit-bourgeois; some of them directly representing interests within the media of the rich. In an era where the traditional bourgeois-democratic notion of "popular support" has been revealed as a farce, it is not enough to even justify the half-assed release of the files as being born from politically consequential public pressure within the proletariat.

Instead, the Epstein case is so peculiar because the evidence that has trickled down throughout the years destroyed the "glamour" around someone who has acquired for themselves a miniature version of the mythology of Hitler. It revealed the real man: stupid, evil, unable to string together a single sentence or use his few neurons to form a coherent thought, but who still manages to occupy his historical role as a power broker solely due to capital. Much like that one photo of Albert Speer issuing orders to concentration camp inmates destroyed the glamour of "Speer the Architect" in a society that otherwise reveres cold and calculating technocracy at the expense of millions of lives, the pictures of Jeffrey Epstein with toddlers destroyed the glamour of "Epstein the Playboy" in a society which is otherwise deeply tolerant of child sexual violence, especially within the bounds of the family.

For the bourgeoisie, it has become a matter of honor. The differing interest groups and parties within that class have come to an agreement that the media cannot be relentlessly bombarded for years with the revelation that, for them, the "thrill" of mass rape is as trivial as a fancy caviar dinner. Best to let it all out now and hold tight for a return to normalcy in the next few years.
