Annex 1

I am writing to you today with a profound sense of urgency regarding the state of American democracy and the need for immediate, peaceful action by the American public. "It is easier to fight for one's principles than to live up to them," and our window for principled action is closing. The very rights and freedoms we cherish—from speech to equality—are perceived by many to be under attack, with fear used as a means of compliance.

​We are calling for the immediate formation of a nationwide "Union of the People" to demonstrate the immense, untapped power of the citizenry and to engage in peaceful, organized resistance. ​Our core proposal centers on preparing for a potential General Strike—a powerful tool that gives the public total control over the national agenda. This is not a call for violence, but a last-chance effort at non-violent, unified resistance.

​To ensure this is a safe, transparent, and viable movement, we propose two critical organizational steps:

​A Secure, Private Registration System: A non-invasive website is needed to allow citizens to register their intent to participate using only a ZIP code, which generates a unique ID number. This protects privacy while allowing us to quantify and organize local support.

​A Mutual Aid Network: A localized social platform or system to connect participants during any potential strike, ensuring essential goods (food, medicine) and aid can be shared within communities.

​The purpose of this national unity is to deliver clear, realistic demands that can restore faith in our democratic process:

​Removal of the Current Administration: A clear, unified demand for the departure of Donald J. Trump and his administration.

​Pause and Review: An immediate pause on all current presidential orders for a full, impartial review. ​Emergency Transition: The appointment of a temporary, non-partisan president until emergency elections can be held to restore constitutional balance.

​I recognize my position is that of an ordinary citizen, but I am willing to stand for our collective freedoms. We need your visibility and influence to help amplify this urgent call to build this union before the opportunity is lost.

​Will you stand with "We the People" in a call for peaceful unity and democratic accountability?
