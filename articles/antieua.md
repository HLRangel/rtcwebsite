The RTC opposes the US aggression against the Venezuelan proletariat, which this Saturday discovered the horrors of "surgical bombings," a favorite tactic of NATO's "humanized wars," responsible for claiming more than 1 million civilian lives [1] in the 21st century alone. The UN spoke a thousand words with its silence, revealing its status as a satellite organization of imperialism and the chief charlatan of "international law," which serves only as a mechanism for the sacrifice of exceptionally cruel individuals on the butchering table of the Law in service to the "national reputation" of the executioners.

We cannot tolerate an immense historical regression that would result in the even more transparent subjugation of the Latin American people to the West, which today dominates our region through large landholdings and financial capital, whether through mineral extraction or gambling houses -- a global cancer that has spread in a way that is especially harmful here.

The "geopolitical support" of small groups like ours is worthless, and therefore we will refrain from developing a complex prescriptive position; we ask that other groups also recognize this, and focus on direct support for the class and its remaining communist organizations in Venezuela, to the extent possible.

LET THE USA FALL!
