DONATE AGAINST GENOCIDE

Redistribute, Transform, Copy!

The RTC is a collective which defends the total abolition of copyright law and analyses its position within the capitalist mode of production. We are the antithesis of groups like the Association of American Publishers and the and the Authors Guild, seeking to educate and lobby against the tyranny of what we call monopolistic rent titles.

We adopt a form of "soft membership", welcoming everyone, from those with an enthusiastic drive to volunteer to those who just want to help on rare occasions. Find out more in our “Contribute” page.
