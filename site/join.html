FAQ

I’m part of a party/organization that forbids double-memberships. Can I participate within the RTC?

That’s not for us to say. However, we certainly don’t conceive of ourselves as a “real” communist organization, that has an exclusive program to whom contributors have a duty to.

Is a regular output expected of those who contribute within the RTC?

No. Although we obviously separate concerns, and those who are only contribute footnotes aren’t given the keys to the vault, we don’t expect anyone to dedicate a significant amount of their time.

Does the RTC collect dues?

No. Financial contributions are completely voluntary with no set date, and no one is expected to contribute.
