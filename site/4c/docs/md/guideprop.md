# GOVERNING STATUTE OF THE REVOLUTIONARY TECHNICAL COLLECTIVE

## On the Nature of the Organization

1. The Revolutionary Technical Collective is a banner of the free association of all communists and communist sympathizers with technical inclinations, founded on the 7th of July 2022, which shall last indefinitely. Its purpose is as follows:

a) To provide technical support for communists and assist in the presence of the communist movement in political life;
    
b) To mount a communist technical association in order to counter the political articulations of the bourgeois technical associations;

c) To promote technical literacy both in our political sphere, and to engage in broader educational efforts ~~whenever~~ where possible.

**d) To theorize about, and to earnestly analyze the contemporary and past conditions of human society.**

**e) To edit, translate, comment, and publish an exemplary set of works within the communist tradition, along with other works that may be of interest to contributors who undertake the task of editing them.**

**e) To seek, as an immediate and historically progressive measure, the most extensive abolition of copyright, patent rights, and trademark rights within the bourgeois-democratic tradition.**

~~Note: When we refer to that which is "technical", we mean all industrial, scientific, artisanal, and political technique.~~

2. A state of normalcy in the organization is constituted by the functioning of the Center of the Revolutionary Technical Collective and by the handling of obligations to its beneficiaries.

## On the Contributors

3. **§1.** A contributor to the Revolutionary Technical Collective is someone who **is over 18 years of age,** participates in its **discussions,** and renders support to the group by assisting in its actions, projects, and in its political development.

**§2. A contributor gains political rights within the group upon asserting them via their contributions, Yearly/Statutory meeting attendance, the unanimous consent of other contributors, or through the consent of the Center.**

**§3. The Collective does not intend to enforce exclusive membership. Whenever a point in this Statute or in a Political Resolution enters in conflict with a fundamental position of another organization a contributor is a part of, an exception to that point is in force for that specific person, on the condition that the other organization complies with the Basic Standards set forth within this Statute.**

**§4. Any employee of a Nocive Entity under Article XX of this Statute shall have all their voting rights and attributions within the Collective extinguished for the foreseeable future, even after the termination of their employment.**

4. **§1** Contributors are expected to act with a great deal of autonomy, engaging and disengaging in action at their own personal discretion, in accordance with this Statute. ~~and asking for the support of the group when desirable and convenient.~~

**§2 Contributors should seek support of the whole group towards their efforts, in a network of mutual reliance, when desirable and convenient.**

## On the Political Resolutions

5. The Political Resolutions are the internal legislative objects of the group~~, reflecting existing measures and intentions~~. **They bind all contributors of the group.** 

6. **§1** The Political Resolutions may be written in any format and conveyed in any manner, including speech.

**§2 When a Political Resolution is conveyed, it is promulgated.**

**§3 Political Resolutions which are unwritten, or which are not written in a descriptive style and/or do not possess a version in a language that can be understood by all contributors they pertain to, shall not take a disciplinary effect if their interpretation varies between all parties possessing good faith.**

**§4 Political Resolutions enter in force when a majority of contributors who maintain a connection to the Official Channels prescribed in this Statute are duly informed of their existence and have access to their text, with a grace period of 7 days being granted for proper digestion of the content, unless otherwise specified.**

7. The scope of the Political Resolutions is **unlimited, with due observation towards the Basic Standards set forth in this Statute**.

## On the Center

8. The Center is the maximum authority of the group. It may:

a) Approve, ammend, reformulate, suspend, or discard this statute;

b) Promulgate and retract Political Resolutions;

c) Evaluate and change the status of contributors.

9. The Center may be a single contributor or a group of contributors.

10. Upon the death, resignation, or incapacity of the Center, its responsibilities devolve to the whole group, which will be in charge of replacing it or reconstituting it.

## On the Yearly Meetings

11. The Yearly Meetings are where all information about the actions of the group for the entire previous year are disseminated, and where new political resolutions are set.

12. While Yearly Meetings are to be preferably spaced by 11-13 months, they may be held on shorter or longer time frames.

## On Conduct

13. **The Collective, as a social environment wherein contributors undertake communal work, should be welcoming to all who engage it in good faith. It is not an institution perpetuated by its contributors but is instead a community of them.** ~~It is expected that all contributors:~~

14. All discrimination on the basis of race, gender, ethnicity, place of birth, disability, and appearance is forbidden.

## Collaboration with Other Groups

**XX. In the spirit of descriptivism and in order to avoid intercenine political conflict, the Collective's standards for collaboration shall concern themselves only with political positions of the bourgeois sort.**

**XX. A group possesses the essential qualities for collaboration with the Collective when they agree that:**

**I. Human beings constitute our should constitute a human community;**

**II. That such a community is based on the mutual care for its social development and on the preservation of the bodily integrity of its members;**

**III. That all those within this community possess or shall possess absolute freedom of self-identification, with all superfluous distinctions of the current world being abolished, replaced by strictly descriptive identifiers.**

**XX. Associated groups which seek to preserve legal relationships in society shall only do so under the banner of complete political equality. It is unacceptable for a group to, for example, defend that the institution of marriage exist only between a man and a woman.**
